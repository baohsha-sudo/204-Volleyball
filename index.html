(About)

Winnipeg's (204) since 2010.

We started 204 Volleyball Club in 2010 and grew into one of the largest clubs in Winnipeg. The 2027 season marks our 18th.

Our teams compete under Volleyball Manitoba, and 204 teams took the court at the 2025 Volleyball Canada Nationals, hosted right here in Winnipeg.
