(About)

Winnipeg's (204) since 2010.

Dan Gilbert and Jared Brown founded 204 Volleyball Club for the 2010 season. They set out to help athletes get recruited for post-secondary volleyball and to prepare them for life after sport.

204 athletes train in the weight room as well as on the court. The club built a strong culture and a real pride in wearing the cardinal and gold. Over the years, 204 teams have won Provincial and National medals.

Our teams compete under Volleyball Manitoba. 2027 marks our 18th season.

(FAQ)

Good to know.

Who plays for 204?+

Boys and girls from 13U through 18U, across 15 teams. Every team competes at the same level.

When and where are tryouts?+

All tryouts run at St. Paul's and cost $40. The tryout form lists every date.

How do I register and pay?+

Fill out the tryout form. All payments go by e-transfer.

Which age group does my athlete play in?+

Birth year decides the age group. Born 2014 or later: 13U. Born 2013: 14U. Born 2012: 15U. Born 2011: 16U. Born 2010: 17U. Born 2009: 18U.

What does a season cost?+

Estimated fees: 13U $1,875. 14U $2,625. 15U and 16U $3,000. 17U and 18U $3,500. The club confirms fees each season at the club meeting in December.

When are camps?+

Summer Camp runs at the end of August at Soul Sanctuary. Fall Camp runs in October and November at Balmoral Hall.

How do I reach a coach?+

Find your team's head coach in the Coaches list, then contact Jared Brown at (204) 996-0675 or b_jared@hotmail.com.
