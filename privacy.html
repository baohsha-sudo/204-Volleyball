Privacy & Terms

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you register through this site, we collect what you enter: the athlete's name and birth year, the program and age group, the parent or guardian's name, an email address, a phone number, and any message you include.

Athletes under 18

Most 204 athletes are minors. Registration goes through a parent or guardian, and we collect only what the club needs to place an athlete on the right team and stay in touch with the family.

Why we collect this information

We use this information to run tryouts, camps, and team placement, and to contact families about club activities. Nothing goes further than this.

How we store your information

Your information stays on file only as long as the club needs the details. We don't sell your information, and we don't share your information with outside parties, beyond what running the club requires, like a registration or payment tool if one is in use, or Volleyball Manitoba where membership calls for athlete details.

Your rights

Ask what information we hold about you or your athlete. Ask us to correct the details, or ask us to delete them, anytime. Reach out using the contact info below.

Cookies and tracking

This site runs no cookies and no tracking scripts of any kind, right now.

Site content

Dates, fees, and team details on this site are for general reference and change from season to season. Contact the club to confirm current details.
